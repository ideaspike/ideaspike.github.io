Learner Coin

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Your family data

Learner Coin stores your parent email account, family name, child nicknames, tasks, notes, coin balances, reward and activity history in its online service. Bank and investment features also store virtual balances, savings activity, simulated orders and positions, quantities, reference values and operation receipts. AWS services support account authentication and storage. Use nicknames for children and avoid putting unnecessary personal information in task or reward text. Nicknames and simulated financial records remain linked to the parent account and are not anonymous.

Parent and child controls

The parent account owns the family. Parent password protection is optional and can be enabled in Family. When enabled, parent controls lock after the configured access period, on switching to a child or when the app enters the background. Account deletion requires password verification. A device or account control does not itself establish that the person using it is a child’s legal guardian. Parents should supervise use and provide any consent required by applicable law.

Optional voice and AI processing

OpenAI processes recordings for transcription and processes instructions for drafting and interpreting requested actions. Depending on the screen, requests include recognized or edited instruction text, necessary child nicknames and identifiers, visible task and reward drafts, Bank draft amounts, and simulated investment context: the selected action and instrument symbol, draft amounts or quantities and quantity-selection mode, child and form identifiers and form revision, the selected position identifier, and visible positions with their identifiers, symbols, long or short direction, quantities and state. The app does not send the entire family database for every request. Review drafts and the visible confirmation before saving or carrying out a simulated financial action. Manual entry remains available when you do not want cloud AI processing. Provider processing may take place outside your country, including the United States.

Provider use and recordings

The beta’s AI notice states that shared inputs and outputs may be used for model improvement and training under the OpenAI project’s sharing settings. Do not assume that AI submissions are excluded from training or retained for zero days. Provider processing and retention are also subject to the applicable OpenAI API data policy. Avoid including children’s personal information or other sensitive information in recordings and instructions. Device recordings are removed after successful transcription, cancellation or leaving the recording flow; the service does not keep the audio recording as a saved family record.

Voice diagnostics and saved actions

During this beta, the service retains recognized and edited instructions, draft context, model settings and action results for troubleshooting, with an automatic-expiry setting of seven days. Voice retry results are available for one hour. Expiry is not a guarantee of physical deletion at that exact moment. Task, reward, Bank or investment records you actually save are separate family records and remain until removed through the available controls or account deletion. Operational diagnostics also record events, request identifiers, status, timing and AI-usage information.

Deleting your account and other requests

In parent mode, open Family and choose Delete Account. The deletion flow removes the login account and associated active family, finance and voice records. A small deletion marker remains to prevent old sessions from recreating deleted data. If deletion is reported as incomplete, retry to finish it or contact support. Deleting the app alone does not delete the online account. Backups and information subject to legal retention obligations may not disappear immediately. Contact support@ideaspike.com for access, correction, deletion or a privacy complaint; we may verify your authority before acting.

Website and Apple services

Opening a website or reference link sends ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to its hosting provider. Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight and device services under its own policies; feedback you choose to send through TestFlight may be visible to IDEASpike. Support correspondence may be retained to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Support and policy updates

You may contact support@ideaspike.com or send beta feedback through TestFlight. Include only information needed to investigate the issue, without passwords, verification codes or unnecessary information about children. We will update this policy when relevant data practices change.
