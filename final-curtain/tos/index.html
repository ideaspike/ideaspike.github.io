Purpose and estimates

Final Curtain helps you reflect on finite time and compare choices. Birth date, life horizon, rates, block durations and end dates are assumptions you control. Estimates are not predictions, commitments or guarantees that you will complete any activity.

Your choices

You decide what matters, whether to change an allocation, and whether to follow any suggestion. Example amounts and population averages are not personal targets. Large blocks replace routine time from today; needs inside those periods are included. References explain comparisons, not an optimal life plan.

Optional on-device summaries can be incomplete or inaccurate. The app does not provide medical, financial or other professional advice, diagnose a person, or determine their worth. For sleep or health concerns, consult an appropriate professional rather than relying on allocation arithmetic.
