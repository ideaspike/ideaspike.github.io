Owl Guard AI Support

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Getting started

Open Owl Guard AI, start the eligible seven-day trial or annual subscription, then use Begin Recording and Apple’s broadcast confirmation. Recording never starts automatically. Supported devices use iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Reviewing evidence

Open Local Evidence beside Settings and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. Review retained screenshots, times, category results and sustained-event groups. The app saves selected screenshots, not a continuous recording.

Subscription, trial and restoration

Eligible new subscribers receive seven days free, then the annual subscription automatically renews at the local price shown by their App Store. The U.S. base price is US$9.99 per year; Apple sets local prices for other regions. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid renewal.

Use Apple’s subscription settings to manage or cancel. Use Restore Purchases when needed. Restoration does not restart an introductory offer. Cancellation leaves access available until the paid period ends; after expiry, new monitoring stops while retained evidence remains available under its retention rules.

Beta alerts

Enable Local alerts (Beta) in Settings if you want to test reminders. A sustained event needs at least 8 hits in 10 valid samples. At a 5-second interval, the first 10 samples take at least 45 seconds. iOS notification settings and Focus may hide or delay reminders. This app cannot block or close other apps.

Existing purchases

Existing one-time full-access purchases remain valid and retain Apple Family Sharing support. Existing non-renewing seven-day trials keep their original deadlines and do not enroll you in a subscription. The new annual subscription is not shared. Evidence stays separately on each device.

Managing storage

In Settings, choose how long to keep recordings (30 days, 90 days or no time limit) and the recording storage limit (200 MB, 500 MB, 1 GB or 2 GB). The default is 30 days and 200 MB. There is no stored-record count limit in updated versions. The oldest records and their saved images are replaced when storage is full. Use Load more in the evidence library to view older entries. Delete All removes both pictures and score-only history after confirmation and device authentication. Earlier builds may use fixed limits; check the controls in your installed version.

Reporting an issue

Please provide the app version, device model, iOS version, access status and the action that failed. You do not need to send private screenshots. Contact support@ideaspike.com. Do not send passwords or private screenshots.
