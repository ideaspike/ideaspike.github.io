Owl Guard AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Screenshots stay on this device

Owl Guard AI analyzes sampled screen images and text locally during a visible, user-authorized system broadcast. Qualifying screenshots and results are kept in its local evidence library. Images are not uploaded, emailed, synced across devices, exported or saved to Photos. No Owl Guard AI registration is required.

Local storage and controls

Recording history and saved images stay in protected storage and are excluded from backups. Updated app versions have no record-count limit. In Settings, choose a retention period (30 days by default, 90 days or no time limit) and a recording storage limit (200 MB by default, 500 MB, 1 GB or 2 GB). Expired records are removed on later access or a sample write. When the selected storage limit is reached, the oldest records and any associated images are replaced. Reducing retention or storage asks for confirmation and device authentication. Delete All removes saved images and score-only history together. Earlier builds used fixed limits; the controls available in your installed version determine its storage policy.

Device authentication

Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode protects evidence viewing and deletion. The app receives an authentication result, not biometric templates. Anyone who can authenticate as the device user can access evidence; this does not establish a separate parent identity.

Optional local alerts (Beta)

Alerts are optional and off by default for new users. Notifications use generic text without evidence images or extracted text. Delivery may be missed or delayed. Evidence review works with alerts disabled.

Apple purchases

Apple handles payments, subscriptions and purchase restoration. Owl Guard AI verifies transactions and renewal status on the device and stores local access status, trial timing and subscription expiry. Screen content is not sent with a purchase.

Eligible subscribers receive a seven-day introductory free trial, then the annual subscription automatically renews at the localized annual price unless cancelled. Manage or cancel through Apple’s subscription settings. Existing one-time full-access purchases and non-renewing trial deadlines remain valid without automatic subscription enrollment. Sharing eligible legacy access never shares evidence.

Data collection

The current app has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK, and no developer-run data-upload service. Apple may process data for its own App Store and operating-system services under Apple policies.

Your private media

Captured evidence can include sensitive content from other apps. Recording is explicitly started by the device user. You can choose monitored categories, stop broadcasting, choose retention and delete evidence inside the app.

Contact

Contact support@ideaspike.com for support or privacy questions. Do not send private screenshots.

This website and support email

These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes normal web requests, which can include an IP address and browser information. These pages do not use advertising or analytics tools. If you email support, we use the message and your email address to respond through our email provider. Do not include private screenshots, passwords or payment details. This optional website or email interaction is separate from the app’s local screenshot processing.
