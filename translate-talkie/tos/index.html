Controlled conversations

Use this pre-release tool for short, supervised conversations between the two intended participants. Make sure each speaker understands that audio is uploaded for AI processing and that the other device receives the result. Do not record others without the permissions required in your circumstances.

Translation limits

This is recorded voice messaging, not continuous simultaneous interpretation. Transcription, translation and synthesized speech may omit words, change meaning or identify the wrong language. Check important meanings with the other participant. Do not rely on it for medical, legal, emergency, financial or confidential decisions.

The current demo room does not verify participant identity. Automatic pairing is not a security guarantee.
