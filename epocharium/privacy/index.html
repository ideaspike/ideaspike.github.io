Epocharium

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The current prototype

Epocharium is a museum-building prototype provided by IDEASpike. The current cloud-connected iOS prototype uses an online account and AWS services to maintain museum progress. This policy describes that prototype; planned production features are not a statement that those features are available.

Account and museum information

AWS Cognito processes your email address, password and verification or recovery requests for authentication. The service associates an account identifier with your museum layout, collection, resources, progression and action receipts. These records allow the app to load your museum and resolve repeated or interrupted actions. The app keeps session information and pending-action information in device storage protected by iOS. Do not send your password or verification codes to support.

Cloud processing and diagnostics

Museum state and action receipts are stored using AWS. Provider processing may take place outside your country, including the United States. Service logs are configured for approximately seven days; this does not set the retention period for account or museum records. Service diagnostics include request or correlation identifiers, the requested operation, status and error information used to operate and troubleshoot the prototype. The current app does not provide a photo-upload or cloud-AI-analysis feature and does not request microphone or location access for museum play. These statements do not cover information you independently include in a support message.

Retention and your choices

Museum and account records remain in the online service while maintained for your account. Signing out or deleting the app does not delete those cloud records. The prototype does not currently include an in-app account-deletion control. Contact support@ideaspike.com to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about retained information. We may need to verify your authority over the account. Deletion from active systems does not necessarily remove copies immediately from backups or records that must be retained under applicable law. Requests are handled subject to applicable privacy law and its requirements.

Apple and purchases

Apple processes App Store and TestFlight interactions under its own terms and privacy practices. The prototype contains simulated game transactions and a StoreKit purchase-testing feature. Simulation is not a real-money purchase. A StoreKit verification result in this prototype does not establish that a production purchase has been fulfilled in your museum. Apple sandbox transactions do not charge real money. We do not receive payment-card details from Apple.

Website and Apple services

Opening a website or reference link sends ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to its hosting provider. Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight and device services under its own policies; feedback you choose to send through TestFlight may be visible to IDEASpike. Support correspondence may be retained to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Support and policy updates

Information you choose to email to support@ideaspike.com is used to address your request. Include only what is needed and omit sensitive account details. You may raise a privacy question or complaint without giving up rights under applicable law. We will update this policy when the relevant data practices change.
