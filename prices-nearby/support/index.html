Good Price Nearby

Photograph a complete product price tag to compare nearby observations. Keep the label readable in the dashed guide; recognition uses the whole photo. A photo without a detected price tag ends the scan.

History and store matches

Scan History reopens saved details and uses a bounded local photo cache to avoid repeat downloads. Photo GPS takes priority over the phone’s current position. Visible store names and branch addresses guide matching. Without photo GPS, the app falls back to phone location and automatically uses the best available supported-store match, including when stores are close together. No store confirmation is required; location warnings are informational. A store match is not proof of the branch.

Get help

Email support@ideaspike.com with the app version and a short description. Include a scan ID only if you want us to investigate that scan. Do not email passwords or sign-in codes. You can also use TestFlight feedback during internal testing.
