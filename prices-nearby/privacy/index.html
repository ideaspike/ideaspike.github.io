Prices Nearby

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What the app does

Prices Nearby reads product price tags and compares nearby price observations. Uploaded photos stay owner-only. When you confirm a scan, its validated product, price, conditions, observation time and public store location may be shared in other users’ comparisons. Your original photo and precise capture coordinates are not shown in those comparisons.

Information we process

We process your email and account identifier for sign-in, the photo you choose or take, capture time and time zone, photo GPS when present, and the phone location available when you capture or select the photo. Recognition, store matches, corrections and feedback are linked to the scan. Service identifiers, errors, processing times and model usage help operate and diagnose the app. Apple product and transaction information, credit purchases, balance changes and funded-result records support purchase delivery, charging and fraud prevention. We do not receive payment-card details from Apple.

Providers and location

AWS supplies authentication, protected photo storage, service infrastructure and store search. Store matching also uses a cached OpenStreetMap store directory, with AWS lookups where needed; location coordinates may be sent to AWS for place and country matching. OpenAI processes a resized, metadata-free copy of the photo, a recognition prompt and a country-derived currency hint when available; the app does not send raw GPS coordinates to the vision model. Removing metadata from that provider copy does not remove visible personal information in the image or the separate location data processed by AWS. We request that OpenAI not store the analysis response; this is not a zero-data-retention or no-training guarantee. Provider processing, retention and optional data-sharing controls follow the applicable policies, including OpenAI API data controls. Processing may occur outside your country, including the United States. Phone location access is optional and used while the app is in use; denying it does not remove GPS information already present in a selected photo. The system photo picker accesses the selected photo rather than requesting the entire library.

Retention and local cache

Cloud photos have a 90-day current-version expiry setting. Older stored versions have a separate 90-day expiry measured from when they become noncurrent, so all versions are not necessarily deleted 90 days after upload. Scan and price metadata have a 365-day expiry setting measured from the scan’s creation. Lifecycle and database cleanup are asynchronous and may take additional time; backups may retain separate copies. Service logs are configured for approximately 30 days. Account, purchase and credit records are separate and may be needed for account operation, delivery, accounting, legal obligations and fraud prevention. A protected, account-separated device cache has a 64 MiB limit, including metadata, with no time-based expiry. When full, it removes the least recently used photos. Local copies may outlive cloud-photo retention until capacity eviction, sign-out, uninstall or operating-system cache reclamation. Cache files and local scan history are excluded from device backups. Signing out or changing accounts clears the previous account’s photo cache; local history references remain account-separated. The app never saves signed download URLs or tokens in that cache, or photos into your Photos library.

Your choices and assistance

You may deny phone location access, select only photos you wish to upload, mark incorrect information and sign out. Taking or choosing a photo starts upload and recognition. Use only photos you have permission to share and avoid including people or unnecessary personal information. Contact support@ideaspike.com for access, correction, or account/cloud-data deletion assistance. This release does not provide an in-app account deletion control. Removing the app removes its local files but does not itself remove cloud records. We may verify your authority before acting on a request. Necessary transaction records and backups may not be removed immediately. Applicable privacy rights remain available.

Optional diagnostic feedback

Choosing Send for diagnosis submits your diagnostic decision, marked issues and any corrections for an already-uploaded scan. This action is separate from the initial photo upload and recognition. Diagnostic reports are not published as confirmed price observations and do not produce a paid comparison. A pending report can remain in the account-separated device outbox and retry when connected; Report saved does not mean the service has received it. Routine service and device diagnostics are separate from this optional feedback.

Other services and updates

The app includes no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Apple’s App Store, TestFlight and device services have their own policies. Feedback you choose to send through TestFlight may be reviewed by IDEASpike. Opening website links sends normal web request information to the destination; information you send to support is used to respond. We update this product-specific policy when processing changes.
