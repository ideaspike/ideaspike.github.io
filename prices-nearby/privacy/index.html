Prices Nearby

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What the app does

Prices Nearby reads product price tags and compares nearby price observations. Uploaded photos stay owner-only. When you confirm a scan, its validated product, price, conditions, observation time and public store location may be shared in other users’ comparisons. Your original photo and precise capture coordinates are not shown in those comparisons.

Information we process

We process your email and account identifier for sign-in, the photo you choose or take, capture time and time zone, photo GPS when present, and the phone location available when you capture or select the photo. Recognition, store matches, corrections and feedback are linked to the scan. Service identifiers, errors, processing times and model usage help operate and diagnose the app. Apple product and transaction information, credit purchases, balance changes and funded-result records support purchase delivery, charging and fraud prevention. We do not receive payment-card details from Apple.

Providers and location

AWS supplies authentication, protected photo storage, service infrastructure and store search. Store matching also uses a cached OpenStreetMap store directory, with AWS lookups where needed; location coordinates may be sent to AWS for place and country matching. OpenAI receives a resized copy of the photo with embedded metadata removed, together with a recognition prompt and a country-derived currency hint when available. Raw GPS coordinates are not sent to the vision model, although visible personal information can remain in the image itself. The app requests that OpenAI not store the analysis response. OpenAI handles API content under its data controls, including abuse-monitoring retention and any project data-sharing settings. Processing may occur outside your country, including the United States. Phone location access is optional and used while the app is in use; denying it does not remove GPS information already present in a selected photo. The system photo picker accesses only the selected photo.

Retention and local cache

Cloud photos are scheduled for cleanup 90 days after upload. A replaced copy is scheduled for cleanup 90 days after it is replaced, so previous copies may remain longer than 90 days from their original upload. Scan and price metadata are scheduled to expire 365 days after the scan is created. Lifecycle and database cleanup can take additional time, and service logs are configured for approximately 30 days. Account, purchase and credit records remain while needed to operate the account, deliver purchases and maintain the credit balance. The device keeps a protected, account-separated photo cache that is excluded from backups and has no time-based expiry; cached photos can remain until capacity cleanup, sign-out, uninstall or operating-system cleanup. Local scan-history references remain separated by account, and the app does not save photos to the Photos library.

Your choices and assistance

You may deny phone location access, select only photos you wish to upload, mark incorrect information and sign out. Taking or choosing a photo starts upload and recognition. Use only photos you have permission to share and avoid including people or unnecessary personal information. Contact support@ideaspike.com to request access, correction, or account and cloud-data deletion. Removing the app clears local files but does not delete online records. We may verify that you control the account and will confirm the outcome of the request. Applicable privacy rights remain available.

Optional diagnostic feedback

Choosing Send for diagnosis submits your diagnostic decision, marked issues and corrections for an already-uploaded scan. This is separate from the original photo upload and recognition. Diagnostic reports are not published as confirmed price observations and do not produce a paid comparison. A pending report may remain in the account-separated device outbox until delivered or removed. Routine service and device diagnostics are separate from this optional feedback.

Other services and updates

The app includes no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Apple’s App Store, TestFlight and device services have their own policies. Feedback you choose to send through TestFlight may be reviewed by IDEASpike. Opening website links sends normal web request information to the destination; information you send to support is used to respond. We update this product-specific policy when processing changes.
